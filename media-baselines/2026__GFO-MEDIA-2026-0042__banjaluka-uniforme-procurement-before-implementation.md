# GFO Media Case Baseline — School Uniforms

## 1. Central claim

A procurement for school uniforms was completed while the public record still showed an unresolved institutional-consent/implementation pathway. The available evidence does not justify the stronger claim that the City acted without any implementation plan; an earlier pilot proposal existed.

A 2 September City PR is treated as a continuing-case communication event, not a second recurrence case.

## 2. Verified facts

- `VERIFIED` — A procurement procedure for school uniforms was initiated on 17 April 2026 and a single acceptable bidder was reported; the reported value was 79,764.75 KM including VAT.
- `VERIFIED` — A contract was reported as signed on 5 August 2026, with one bidder and the same total value.
- `VERIFIED` — By 25 August the City publicly stated that 450 uniform sets had been secured for pupils in grades 1–3 and that implementation had not yet taken effect because consent from schools/Ministry was required.
- `VERIFIED` — An earlier April public record described a pilot concept for about 300 pupils in two schools and stated that Ministry consent had not yet been obtained.
- `VERIFIED` — On 1 September 2026 the Ministry publicly stated that it had not granted consent, that it had requested additional justification/documentation concerning pedagogical justification, implementation, financing and evaluation, and that the requested supplementation had not been supplied by that date.
- `VERIFIED` — On 2 September 2026 the City published a PR framing the lack of consent primarily as Ministry disinterest/lack of cooperation and juxtaposing that issue with betting shops near schools and unhealthy products; the PR did not include the Ministry's stated documentation/pedagogical explanation from the previous day.

## 3. Supported inferences

- `SUPPORTED INFERENCE` — The evidence supports a process gap between procurement/contracting and a completed institutional approval path for implementation. It does not by itself establish illegality, waste, bad faith or intent.
- `SUPPORTED INFERENCE` — The shift from an earlier public pilot figure of about 300 pupils to 450 sets is a material change requiring documentary explanation, but is not itself evidence of irregularity.
- `SUPPORTED INFERENCE` — The 2 September PR gives an incomplete explanation of non-consent because a materially relevant and recent Ministry explanation is omitted.

## 4. Unresolved / disputed facts

- `UNRESOLVED` — Exact documentary status of City-school-Ministry communications before 17 April and before contract signature on 5 August.
- `UNRESOLVED` — Full delivery, formal acceptance and payment status at each relevant date.
- `UNRESOLVED` — Documentary basis for quantity 450 and final intended schools/pupils.
- `DISPUTED` — Responsibility for failure to implement remains contested between City and Ministry.

## 5. Key omissions / information gaps

- Formal consent/approval chain before procurement and contract signature.
- Complete procurement file and technical quantity justification.
- Delivery/acceptance/payment documentation.
- Full correspondence showing what the Ministry requested and what the City subsequently submitted.
- In the 2 September PR specifically: the Ministry's public explanation that additional project justification/documentation remained outstanding.

## 11. Publication-safe wording

`The City PR accurately reflects that Ministry consent was absent, but it presents the dispute primarily as lack of interest or cooperation. A public Ministry statement from the previous day gave a materially different explanation: the pilot proposal required additional justification and documentation which, according to the Ministry, had not been supplied. Omitting that explanation supports a finding of selective framing/incomplete context, not a conclusion about unlawful conduct or intent.`
