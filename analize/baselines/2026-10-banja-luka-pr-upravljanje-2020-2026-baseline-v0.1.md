# GF Forensic Baseline — Banja Luka institutional PR and governance 2020–2026

Status: WORKING BASELINE v0.1
Date: 2026-10-04
Scope: City of Banja Luka; mayor Draško Stanivuković; period from assumption of office after 2020 local elections through 2026 general-election campaign.
Purpose: reusable evidentiary baseline for future GF analyses. This is not a finding of illegality or intent.

## Central research question

Do records from 2020–2026 show a longitudinal transformation in which City-funded policies, projects, benefits and urban interventions are increasingly personalized around the mayor and later reused as political proof of capacity to govern at the Republika Srpska level?

Formal election-campaign dates are one phase of the analysis, not the temporal boundary of political communication.

## Evidence rules

Separate:
1. documented institutional act;
2. source/actor claim;
3. analytical indication;
4. unverified hypothesis.

Absence of a public record is not evidence that a record does not exist.
Election timing alone is not proof of electoral intent.
A legitimate public policy can simultaneously create political capital.
Legal qualification requires primary legal/administrative evidence.
Dismissed/closed proceedings must be reported with equal prominence to the original allegation.

## Longitudinal model

PUBLIC RESOURCE/POLICY
→ institutional decision
→ procurement/implementation
→ citizen-facing result
→ institutional PR
→ personalization around office-holder
→ accumulated political capital
→ reuse in party/campaign narrative
→ projection of local-government record to RS-level political ambition.

## Preliminary periodization

### Phase 1 — 2020/2021: transparency and “new administration”
Early communication strongly foregrounded transparency and the mayor personally. City communications in January 2021 described the mayor's work as “completely transparent”. Early mandate included publication of employee information and public presentation of transparency measures.

Test: establish baseline PR frequency and share of mayor-centered posts.

### Phase 2 — 2022: benefits + ceremonial delivery
City PR repeatedly frames benefits as first/important/historic achievements and places the mayor at delivery events.
Examples:
- free electronic public-transport cards for students/pupils from 4+ families;
- free transport for lowest-pension pensioners;
- housing support for large families;
- 1,000 KM support to 300+ unemployed children of fallen soldiers.
Relevant pattern: policy → direct beneficiary → ceremonial handover → mayor quote → achievement narrative.

### Phase 3 — 2023: consolidated “mandate results”
City's three-year mandate summary aggregates benefits and capital projects and states that more than 50m KM from the city treasury supported numerous citizen benefits. Communication explicitly packages disparate policies as results of the mayoral administration.
Test denominator/methodology behind aggregate amounts and distinguish expenditure, foregone fees, subsidies and capital investment.

### Phase 4 — 2024: local-election stress test
Analyze continuity between institutional and campaign channels, including personnel overlap, public events, benefits and reuse of City-produced material.
Claims about coercion of employees must remain attributed unless supported by independent/primary evidence.
Use CIK/court decisions where available.

### Phase 5 — 2025/2026: urban transformation + accountability gap
Vrbas riverbank interventions become a high-value transparency case. On 2026-04-03, District Court Banja Luka ordered the City to decide on the Center for Environment's 2025-07-04 FOI request after administrative silence.
This creates a documented contrast with the early transparency narrative.
Track riverbank project: initiation → design → planning basis → permits → procurement → contract → amendments → payment → environmental/urban outcome.

### Phase 6 — 2026: local record projected to RS level
PSS campaign communication uses Stanivuković's Banja Luka record as evidence of governing capacity. Stanivuković is a candidate/list carrier for NSRS IJ3; “premier” is a political ambition/narrative, not a directly elected office.
Test provenance: which City achievements/statistics/images/formulations reappear in political channels.

## Governance anomaly cluster: authorized signatories

In early 2021 the mayor authorized 13 employees to sign acts from departments rather than proceeding through the conventional Assembly appointment of department heads. City stated legal basis existed in the Local Self-Government Law and City Statute; political opponents and some lawyers disputed that interpretation.

This must be reconstructed from primary acts.

Longitudinal test:
- for each department/year 2021–2026 identify formally appointed head, acting head, or authorized signatory;
- authority instrument and duration;
- who exercised managerial vs signature powers;
- Assembly proposals/votes;
- audit or court findings;
- effect on traceability of responsibility.

Do not state “no department head was appointed 2020–2026” until verified department-by-department. A 2024 public dispute indicates the issue persisted, but is not sufficient alone to prove uninterrupted status.

## Financial/urban anomaly clusters

### Roundabouts and major roads
Create project ledger:
project → investor → financier → procurement authority → contractor → initial value → amendments → final payment → permit/planning basis → opening/PR attribution.

A criminal investigation concerning approximately 1.77m KM credit expenditure for the Ekvator roundabout was reportedly discontinued in 2026; this closure must accompany any reference to the original allegation.

### Donations / “gifts to the City”
For each donation:
donor → asset/service → market value → agreement → procurement implications → donor's City contracts/urban interests → maintenance/follow-on City cost.
Analytical question: distinguish acquisition price (“free”) from total cost of ownership.

### Free/subsidized public transport
Track:
eligible population → issued cards → active users → rides → City subsidy → carrier compensation → baseline before measure → methodology behind claims such as “tripled users”.
Separate access/output from mobility outcome.

### Vrbas / changing the face of the city
Highest-priority urban provenance case.
Track:
old condition → initiator → professional design → public consultation → regulatory plan → permits → procurement → contractor → amendments → final cost → ecological monitoring → completed condition.
Documented anchor: District Court Banja Luka judgment 11 0 U 040532 25 U, 2026-04-03, ordering City action on FOI request after administrative silence.

### Other large public-space transformations
Apply same provenance chain to parks, monuments, central zone, riverbank, roundabouts and projects attributed to any informal/“creative team”.
Key question: who made the institutional decision and where is the decision trail?

## PR corpus methodology v0.1

Corpus: official City website/archive, 2021–2026 (2020 transition separately).

For every post record:
date; title; category; mayor named in title Y/N; mayor central photo Y/N; direct quote Y/N; “historic/record/first/free/gift/our measures/our administration/mandate” lexicon; benefit handover Y/N; infrastructure opening/visit Y/N; monetary amount; named beneficiary group; City vs mayor attribution; campaign-channel reuse Y/N.

Annual outputs:
- total PR posts;
- posts/month;
- mayor-centered share;
- benefit/ceremony share;
- “free/historic/record” rate;
- public-money amount mentioned;
- share with identifiable decision/procurement/result provenance;
- campaign reuse rate.

Important: current v0.1 establishes the coding framework and verified anchor examples. It does NOT yet claim a statistically demonstrated increase in PR frequency. That claim requires exhaustive annual corpus collection.

- 2022-03: free electronic transport cards begin for students/pupils from 4+ families; City calls it first-time/significant.
- 2022-08: free transport announced for lowest-pension pensioners; described as an important/historic decision and fulfillment of promises.

- 2023 mandate summary: City packages free/subsidized measures and projects as three-year administration results; states >50m KM in citizen benefits.

## Baseline conclusion

The evidence already supports a longitudinal inquiry into personalization of City achievements and the conversion of institutional outputs into political narrative. It also identifies a documented transparency tension and several governance/provenance questions. The evidence does not yet support a quantitative claim that PR frequency increased over the full 2020–2026 period, nor a blanket causal claim that individual City measures were created for a future premiership campaign. Those are testable hypotheses for the next corpus/data phase.
